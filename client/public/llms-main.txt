# SyncStock

> Restock alerts for sneaker, streetwear and trading card stores on Shopify. Shoppers get notified the moment their size is back, and the store sees exactly what to restock next.

SyncStock is a Shopify app. It syncs a store's catalog automatically and gives the store its own shopper site (storename.syncstock.io), where shoppers browse what's in stock, save their sizes and set alerts. When a matching item is back, SyncStock notifies them by email, browser push or the phone app. One plan for any Shopify store: $50/month, unlimited restock alerts, 7-day free trial.

## Who it's for

- [Sneaker stores](https://www.syncstock.io/for/sneaker-stores): size-matched alerts, so a 10.5 restock reaches the people who wear a 10.5; brand new or pre-owned; see which sizes to source next
- [Streetwear stores](https://www.syncstock.io/for/streetwear-stores): letter sizes, price-drop alerts, browse by brand and price
- [Trading card shops](https://www.syncstock.io/for/trading-card-shops): alerts by raw condition, grading company and lowest grade, or factory-sealed only
